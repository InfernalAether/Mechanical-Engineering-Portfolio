INTRODUCTION

About Me

I'm Andrew Huang, a Mechanical Engineering student at San Francisco State University. I graduated from Chaffey College in May 2025 with an Associate's degree in Engineering.

My interest in engineering comes from a curiosity about how things work and how mathematical and scientific principles can be applied to real-world problems. I've always enjoyed building, understanding, and improving mechanical systems.

My primary interests include robotics, mechanical design, and simulation. Through academic coursework and independent projects, I've been developing my skills in CAD, programming, and engineering analysis.

This portfolio documents my engineering projects, technical development, and experience as I continue working toward a career in engineering.

Outside engineering, I enjoy tennis, gaming, and learning new skills.
